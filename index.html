Explore AI capabilities

See what they can do

From everyday conversations to advanced desktop tools, explore the capabilities we aim to bring to the Darkstar AI collection. Features depend on the individual assistant and release.

💬

Natural conversation

Understand everyday questions, follow context, explain complex topics, and hold useful conversations.

🧠

Memory & personalisation

Remember approved preferences, ongoing projects, and useful context with controls to review or clear saved data.

🌐

Web research

Search current information, compare sources, summarise findings, and provide links to evidence.

🎙️

Voice & accessibility

Optional speech recognition, spoken replies, wake-word support, and accessible keyboard-friendly controls.

🖥️

Desktop assistance

Open installed apps, find files, help organise folders, and perform approved Windows tasks.

⚙️

Automation & workflows

Create reminders, automate repeat tasks, and manage multi-step workflows with appropriate permissions.

✍️

Writing & creativity

Draft emails, stories, scripts, social posts, presentations, and creative ideas.

💻

Coding & troubleshooting

Write, explain, review, and debug code; assist with websites, software, and technical problems.

📄

Files & documents

Read and summarise supported PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, and notes when users provide access.

🖼️

Images & media

Understand supplied images and support image generation or editing where enabled.

📅

Planning & productivity

Help with schedules, study plans, to-do lists, research notes, and project organisation.

🔌

Integrations & extensions

Connect to supported tools and services with permission; expand capabilities through updates.

🔒

Privacy & security

Clear permission prompts, safe handling of personal data, secure updates, and control over stored information.

🛡️

Safety & transparency

Explain limitations, ask before sensitive actions, avoid fabricated claims, and show when information is uncertain.

🌍

Languages & learning

Translate, tutor, explain step by step, and adapt answers to different experience levels.

🚀

Performance & reliability

Responsive interface, clear error messages, updates, recovery options, and compatibility information.

Capabilities vary by AI

These are planned and potential capabilities, not a promise that every listed feature is already available. Check each AI’s release page for its confirmed features, requirements, limitations, and privacy details.
