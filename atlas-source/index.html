TO SPEAK TO ATLAS, SAY “ATLAS” FIRST
ϟ   QUICK COMMANDS
♫ ATLAS MUSIC

ATLAS automatically finds songs in your Windows Music folder and ATLAS Music folder. You can also add other songs or folders. Say “ATLAS, play [song name]”.

No music selected
Spotify Free: paste a Spotify song link to load the official player inside ATLAS. To find a song by name, use SEARCH SPOTIFY. Full-song availability is controlled by Spotify.
♫ SPOTIFY · INSIDE ATLAS
Spotify controls which songs can play in full on a Free account. Sign in within the Spotify player if prompted.
▶ YOUTUBE PLAYER · INSIDE ATLAS
Search by song name above. YouTube may require you to press Play or may block some music videos.
Music in your Windows Music folder or ATLAS-Darkstar/Music is available automatically each time ATLAS starts. Other manually selected files need selecting again after restarting. Spotify playback uses Spotify’s official embedded player; availability and login are controlled by Spotify.
▣ SCREEN VISION

Share a screen or window only when you choose. ATLAS receives individual frames only when you press Analyze. Stop sharing whenever you want.

Requires Ollama vision model (default: llama3.2-vision). No automatic recording or continuous uploads.
▦ INSTALLED APPLICATIONS

Find installed Windows applications. Click one to launch it. Only applications discovered on your PC are eligible.
